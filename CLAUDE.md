
## Frontend Design

For every frontend/UI/UX task:

1. Read `/DESIGN.md` first. It is the primary project design system.
2. Read `.claude/skills/frontend-design/SKILL.md` before designing or modifying UI.
3. Treat `docs/design-references/apple.DESIGN.md` and
   `docs/design-references/claude.DESIGN.md` as secondary references only.
4. Do not mix reference design systems arbitrarily.
5. Avoid generic AI/SaaS aesthetics: excessive gradients, glow,
   glassmorphism, pills, floating cards, and decorative animations.
6. Existing product identity and project-specific design decisions override
   generic recommendations from the frontend-design skill.
